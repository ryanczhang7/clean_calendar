## Decided

1. **The mutation gate as configured is not an instrument on this host. Do
   not run it unscoped.** `bash scripts/gates.sh --gate mutation` instruments
   58 files and 3,608 mutants, starts 11 runners, and scored the first 22
   mutants **all Timeout** (counted as detected by Stryker) in about 6
   minutes, projecting 16 h 23 min. I stopped it. Its evidence line in
   `project.conf` is still UNVERIFIED: the run never reached the clear-text
   table it matches. Run Stryker per module, as this audit and EPIC-01 did,
   with a scratch config, `concurrency: 6` and a narrowed command first.
   Rests on E-GATE. I have not proved why every mutant timed out. Load from
   11 concurrent full-suite runs is the hypothesis E-GATE is consistent
   with.
2. **The pure date modules are well pinned *on this machine*, and none of
   that DST protection exists on CI.** Only `moveEvent.test.ts` pins
   `TZ=America/Chicago`. `monthGrid.test.ts`, `timeGrid.test.ts` and
   `period.test.ts` run in the host's zone. The host is Chicago and CI is
   `ubuntu-latest` (UTC). Three realistic DST defects (building days by
   adding 24 h of milliseconds in `monthGrid`, `timeGrid.daysFrom` and
   `period`'s `addDays`) are killed here and **survive the whole unit suite
   under `TZ=UTC`** (1603/1603 each). Locally recorded gate runs catch them,
   CI does not, and a developer on a UTC or non-DST machine would not
   either. Filed as **CAL-039**. Rests on E-DST.
3. **Chip drag only works rightward and downward as far as the tests know.**
   Removing `Math.abs` from the drag threshold means a chip dragged left or
   up never starts a drag (no move to an earlier day or an earlier time).
   The whole unit suite passes (1603/1603): every drag in every suite moves
   by a positive dx or dy. Also unpinned: the `pointermove` and `keydown`
   listeners being removed when a press ends, a click with no pointerdown
   before it (keyboard Enter) not being swallowed, and only Escape
   cancelling a drag. Filed as **CAL-040**. Rests on E-HAND H6, H7, H8 and
   E-STRYKER `useChipDrag.ts`.
4. **The page's keyboard moves and undo outcome have real holes.** A
   successful undo that shows the "couldn't save" alert anyway (`:182`)
   survives. So does an `Alt+↑` that moves the event *down* (`:127`), or
   does nothing and throws (`:125`). AC-3 of CAL-013 pins only `Alt+→`, and
   no test presses `Alt+↑` in any view. Also unpinned: focus after a second
   keyboard move (`:163`), the `?.` that keeps a move out of the visible
   period from throwing in an effect (`:170`), and an all-day `Alt+↑` in a
   time grid (`:132`). Filed as **CAL-041**. Rests on E-STRYKER
   `CalendarPage.tsx` and E-HAND H16, H17.
5. **The page's selection and history handling are unpinned in four
   places:** the year in `sameMonth` (`:80`), a new history entry on every
   in-month arrow key (`:294`), the shared selection not re-synced on a
   period change (`:357`, `:366`), and the opener that `N` and Add hand the
   sheet for focus return (`:83`). Filed as **CAL-042**. Rests on
   E-STRYKER `CalendarPage.tsx` and E-HAND H18, H20.
6. **Clearing a date or time field in the event form is untested, and its
   only protection is a guard that can be deleted silently.** With
   `moveStart`'s validity guard removed, clearing the start date throws
   `RangeError: Invalid time value` from the change handler. With `local()`'s
   null guard removed, `moveStart` and `validate` throw `TypeError`. 156/156
   form tests pass either way. Also unpinned: an all-day value whose end is
   before its start (`:137`), and whitespace-only location or notes stored as
   typed (`:193`). Filed as **CAL-043**. Rests on E-STRYKER `eventForm.ts`
   and E-HAND H13 plus E-CLEAR.
7. **The write paths and the stories' central claims hold.**
   `eventActions.ts` 16/16 killed. `eventsStore.ts` `hide`/reveal and counts
   87/89 (2 equivalent or acceptable). `events.ts`'s new `restore`: all
   killed, and the 3 survivors are EPIC-01's equivalent `order`/`select`
   mutants. `moveEvent.ts` 35/37 (2 equivalent). The undo token (H9), the
   restore row (H10), the same-day-drop guard (H11) and an early reveal (H14)
   were all killed. Nothing here asks for these to be retested.
8. **"Once-only undo" is enforced by the toast dismissing itself, not by
   the two guards named for it.** CalendarPage's `once()` and Toast's
   `done` flag can be removed **together**, and both suites stay green
   (138/138, H19). The cases "an undo runs once: Ctrl+Z after Undo writes
   nothing more" (`CalendarPage.test.tsx:1798`) and "a second Ctrl+Z after
   the first does nothing more" (`Toast.test.tsx:213`) pass through
   dismissal. Their names claim more than they pin. The behaviour holds
   today, so the guards are classed acceptable. CAL-041 AC-7 pins the page
   guard directly, so a future toast that stays visible (CAL-013 PO
   decision 6's pause-on-hover) cannot quietly make undo repeatable.
9. **`WalkthroughPage.test.tsx` times out under parallel load too, and that
   produced a false kill.** Under 6 concurrent full-suite runs, its cases hit
   the 5,000 ms per-test timeout (19 failures in one run, 5 in another, 1 in
   a third). One of those runs was the only "kill" of a CalendarPage
   survivor (`:80:3`). Re-run against that suite and the page's, it passes
   172/172. CAL-038 covers only `SignInPage.test.tsx`. **Not filed here**:
   it is not a survivor cluster. It is recommended to the orchestrator,
   because it makes full-suite mutation kills untrustworthy in the same way
   CAL-038's flake does. Rests on E-FLAKE.
10. **No CAL-009 to CAL-013 assertion that passed on arrival is still
    unearned.** CAL-009 had none with an assertion. CAL-010, CAL-011,
    CAL-012 and CAL-013 each pasted a probe. CAL-011's three absence needles
    were paid in GREEN. CAL-012's two markup-equality cases were earned in RED
    by a probe that showed only that the comparison discriminates, since
    `onActivate` was ignored then. H12 now earns the chip one against the
    shipped prop.

false survivals, so every survivor below stands. Every kill that later

### E-STRYKER: survivors, by module

Notation: `file:line:col`, original → mutant. Classes: **equivalent** (no
observable difference), **acceptable** (observable, but not worth a test,
with the reason), **gap** (should be killed).

**`src/domain/moveEvent.ts`**: 2 equivalent.
- `:36:7` `if (event.allDay)` → `false` in `moveToDay`. The timed path builds
  `day` at the event's local time (00:00 for an all-day event), and
  `moveEvent`'s all-day branch truncates it to that day's midnight anyway.
  Hours are 0 to 23, so the date cannot roll over.
- `:52:7` the same in `moveToSlot`. `moveEvent`'s all-day branch takes
  `atDay(slotStart)`, which is what `moveToDay(event, slotStart)` gives.

**`src/domain/eventForm.ts`**: 14 gap, 4 acceptable, 2 equivalent.

  geometry: `fireEvent.pointerMove` on `document` reaches the hook as a
  browser's would, and a keyboard Enter on a `<button>` fires `click` with
  no pointer events (the H8 and `:135` argument).

  and flushes a discrete update before the next input task (the equivalence
  and acceptability of the `done`, `once`, `mounted` and listener-leak
  survivors).
- date-fns' `differenceInDays` compares wall-clock time across DST. Measured

  not re-checked).
- `<input type="date">` and `<input type="time">` (no `step`) never produce
  any value other than the anchored shapes or `""` (the regex-anchor
  survivors).
- Stryker's command runner treats any non-zero exit as Killed. A kill
