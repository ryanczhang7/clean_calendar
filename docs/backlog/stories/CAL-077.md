## Context

EPIC-06 (recurring events). A follow-up to CAL-075, filed on the user's
decision of 2026-11-19 ("File a follow-up story") after CAL-075's diff review,
round 1, "Should fix 1". Two symptoms of one wrong assumption: after "All
events in the series" moves a series by whole days, the app takes the
occurrence the owner acted on to have moved by that many days too. That is
true of a `daily` or `weekly` series. It is not true of a `yearly` one:
`expand` (`shared/recurrence.ts`, `shift`) re-anchors every occurrence on the
series row's month and day, so when the move carries the series across the
end of February, an occurrence in a year whose February is a different
length lands one day away from "old date plus days".

1. **Focus.** `movedOccurrence` in `src/calendar/CalendarPage.tsx` (CAL-075
   Block 3) keys the chip to refocus as the old `occurrenceDate` plus `days`.
   For the yearly case that key names no chip and focus is lost.
2. **Toast.** The toast text is fixed before the scope is chosen:
   `commandSpan` (`CalendarPage.tsx`) calls `movedLabel(span, event.allDay)`
   (`src/domain/moveEvent.ts`) on the span ASKED FOR, the shown occurrence
   plus the key's days. `confirmScope` passes that `text` to `written`
   unchanged in the series branch, while the write is `seriesSpan(series,
   event, span)`. So the toast names the asked date, and the occurrence is
   drawn a day later.

Both were reproduced at PLANNED by running the real `expand`, `moveToDay`,
`seriesSpan` and `movedLabel` (Notes has the output). One behaviour, one
cycle: both halves need the same fact - where the acted-on occurrence lands -
and Contract Block 3 gives it one home.

**Not a regression.** `main` before CAL-075 lost focus in the same case
(focus went by the pre-move key, which names no chip either), and the toast
has named the asked date since CAL-025.

This is a `fix`: AC-1 to AC-4 each reproduce a defect. The required gate that
fails if it breaks is `unit` (and `coverage`): jsdom tests in
`src/calendar/CalendarPage.test.tsx`, and RED may add direct cases to
`src/domain/moveEvent.test.ts`. No optional gate is needed.

Constraint: v1 assumes the device's zone (`docs/wiki/product-brief.md`, "Time
zones"; `docs/wiki/architecture.md`, "Dates"). Every fixture's series is in
the device zone (`tz: DEVICE_TZ`).

## Acceptance criteria

All dates are local to the device zone, and each series' `recurrence.tz` is
the device zone. 2028 is a leap year; 2026 is not.

- **AC-1** — Given an all-day yearly series "Birthday" that starts on
  25 February 2026, in the month view of February 2028 (`/month/2028-02`),
  when the owner focuses its chip on Friday 25 February 2028, presses
  Alt+ArrowDown (seven days) and confirms "All events in the series", then
  keyboard focus is on the Birthday chip in Saturday 4 March 2028's cell.
  (Today focus is on no chip: the series row becomes 4 March 2026, the 2028
  occurrence is keyed `@2028-03-04`, and the code looks for `@2028-03-03`.)
- **AC-2** — Given AC-1's fixture and move, when the move is confirmed "All
  events in the series", then the toast reads "Moved to Sat 4 Mar", with its
  Undo. (Today it reads "Moved to Fri 3 Mar", a day on which no Birthday
  chip is drawn.)
- **AC-3** — Given an all-day yearly series "Birthday" that starts on
  28 February 2026, in the month view of February 2028, when the owner
  focuses its chip on Monday 28 February 2028, presses Alt+ArrowRight (one
  day) and confirms "All events in the series", then keyboard focus is on
  the Birthday chip in Wednesday 1 March 2028's cell. (Today focus is on no
  chip: the series row becomes 1 March 2026, the 2028 occurrence is keyed
  `@2028-03-01`, and the code looks for `@2028-02-29`.)
- **AC-4** — Given AC-3's fixture and move, when the move is confirmed "All
  events in the series", then the toast reads "Moved to Wed 1 Mar", with its
  Undo. (Today it reads "Moved to Tue 29 Feb".)
- **AC-5** — Given AC-1's fixture (the chip on Friday 25 February 2028
  focused, Alt+ArrowDown), when the move is confirmed "This event only"
  instead, then the toast reads "Moved to Fri 3 Mar", with its Undo, and the
  Birthday chip is in Friday 3 March 2028's cell - as today. A regression
  guard: only a whole-series move changes what the toast names.

**Block 2 - where the acted-on occurrence lands.** After "All events in the
series", occurrence number `k` of the old series is occurrence number `k` of
the moved series. For `daily` and `weekly` that is the asked-for start (the
old start plus the key's days), which is what CAL-075 Block 2 already says
and stays as it is. For `yearly`, `k` is the occurrence's local year minus
the series row's local year, and the landing start is the MOVED series row's
local month, day and time of day in the year `moved series year + k`. AC-1:
series 25 Feb 2026, occurrence 2028 (`k` = 2), moved series 4 Mar 2026,
landing 4 Mar 2028 - not 25 Feb 2028 + 7 = 3 Mar 2028, because February 2028
has 29 days and February 2026 has 28. The same rule moves the landing a day
EARLIER than asked going the other way (measured: series 4 Mar 2026, shown
4 Mar 2028, Alt+ArrowUp asks 26 Feb 2028, the series becomes 25 Feb 2026 and
the occurrence lands 25 Feb 2028); RED may pin that at the domain level.
