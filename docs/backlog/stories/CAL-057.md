## Context

EPIC-07, brief §4.6. Split out of CAL-026 on 2026-11-03 (CAL-026, Notes, PO
decision 1, the owner's choice): CAL-026 is the sync layer for `reminders`
(`ReminderActions` `setReminder` / `clearReminder` over the outbox, the
drain, catch-up, `useReminder(eventId)`, and `deleted_at` on the table);
this story is what the owner sees on top of it - the sheet's Reminder field
(`components.md`, "Event sheet", field 8), saving it with the event, and the
chip's bell ("Event chip", Reminder variant; `accessibility.md`, the chip's
name). Its criteria are CAL-026's original AC-1, AC-2, AC-4 and AC-5, with
the form half of its AC-3. `touches:` is a first estimate; the contract,
written at this story's PLANNED, settles it.

Required gate that would fail if this broke: `unit`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given the form, when the Reminder select is opened, then it offers
  None, At time, 5, 10, 15 and 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours and 1 day before,
  mapping to `minutes_before` null, 0, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, 120, 1440.
- **AC-2** - Given a new event saved with "30 minutes before", when it is
  written, then the outbox holds the event op followed by a `reminders` insert
  with `minutes_before = 30` and `occurrence_date = null`.
- **AC-3** - Given an event with a reminder, when its sheet opens the field
  shows that reminder; when it is changed and saved `setReminder` is called
  with the new value, and when it is set to None `clearReminder` is called; a
  change to the reminder alone is a change the sheet saves.
- **AC-4** - Given an all-day event, when "At time" is chosen, then the helper
  reads "At 09:00 on the day" and the stored `minutes_before` is `-540`
  (09:00 after the event's local midnight start).
- **AC-5** - Given an event with a live reminder, when its chip renders, then a
  bell glyph shows and the accessible name includes ", reminder 30 minutes
  before" (", reminder at start time" for 0; ", reminder 1 day before").

## Notes

- Created 2026-11-03 by the lead-po orchestrator (Fable 5.1) when CAL-026
  was split (CAL-026, Notes, PO decision 1).
- **Carried from CAL-026 - all-day "At time".** `components.md` says an
  all-day event's "At time" means 09:00 on the day. Storing it as a negative
  `minutes_before` (`-540`) keeps the scheduler's rule a single formula,
  `fire_at = start - minutes_before`, for every event.
- **Open for this story's PLANNED - a reminder on an occurrence.** An
  override cannot carry a reminder, and the template belongs to the series.
  The likely answer mirrors CAL-025 PO decision 4 (a repeat-rule change is
  always a series change, with no edit-scope dialog), but it is a product
  decision for the owner, not settled here.
