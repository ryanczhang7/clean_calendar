- **`:169`** (AC-1's `posted.mime` is `image/png`) and **`:228-230`** (AC-3's
  posted base64 is non-empty) are likewise unearned through the real canvas.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given the AC-2 case of `e2e/downscale.spec.ts` (a 1200 x 800
  JPEG), then no assertion in it compares a value with itself: the
  self-comparison at `:210-212` is removed or replaced by a check against an
  independent value, and the story's `## Notes` says which and why.
- **AC-2** - Given the same case, when the canvas is asked to encode PNG while
  the request is still labelled JPEG (`src/capture/downscale.ts`
  `69s#mime,#"image/png",#`), then the AC-2 case fails on its JPEG marker
  check, the failure output shows both the first two bytes (`[137, 80]`
  where `[255, 216]` is expected) and the last two (`[96, 130]` where
  `[255, 217]` is expected), and the AC-1 and AC-3 cases still pass.
- **AC-3** - Given the AC-1 case (a 3000 x 1000 PNG), when the page posts a
  wrong `mime` label (`src/capture/CapturePage.tsx`
  `153s#base64, mime }#base64, mime: "image/jpeg" }#`), then the AC-1 case
  fails on its `posted.mime` check.
- **AC-4** - Given the AC-3 case, when the encoder resolves an empty blob
  (`src/capture/downscale.ts` `68s#resolve(blob)#resolve(new Blob([]))#`),
  then the AC-3 case fails on its non-empty check, not on the 10 s wait.
