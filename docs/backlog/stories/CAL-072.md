## Context

EPIC-07. Follow-up to CAL-057 (the event sheet's Reminder field), filed at
the user's request ("Yes, file it") from CAL-057's `## Diff review`, review 4
(`Verdict: Ship.`, no code fix owed) and review 1's Nice to have 2. Line
numbers are those of `main` at `f393324`.

CAL-057 Block 4's three amendments settle how an "At time" reminder (0 timed,
-540 all-day) behaves on a repeating event, where the reminder is the
series' one template but a single date can be overridden to all-day. Two
lines that carry that rule have no test that fails when they are wrong:

- **The "This event only" basis** (`src/events/EventSheet.tsx:415`, `const
  basis = thisOnly ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay;`). Review 4's probe
  `s/thisOnly ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay/sheetAllDay/` passed 136/136. The
  unpinned input: a timed series (15-minute template), a timed date, All day
  switched on, "At time" picked, "This event only" - today
  `setReminder(series, 0)`; mutated, -540, so every other timed date reminds 9
  hours after its start.
- **The helper's basis after an All-day switch on an occurrence**
  (`EventSheet.tsx:602`, `allDay: sheetAllDay,`). Review 4's probe keeping the
  stored series basis on an occurrence passed 269/269 under `src/events`. A
  timed date of an "At time" series with All day switched on could lose its
  "At 09:00 on the day" helper, or keep one when switched back, with nothing
  red. (The plain-event basis is pinned: `allDay: storedAllDay` fails 2 at
  `EventSheet.test.tsx:2420`.)

And one cleanup review 1 asked for: the live-template rule (`occurrenceDate
=== null && deletedAt == null`) is written twice - `liveTemplate`
(`src/sync/remindersStore.ts:29-41`) and the page's map
(`src/calendar/CalendarPage.tsx:531-541`). If one changes and the other does
not, the chip's bell and the sheet's field disagree about the same event.

**Open question for the PO (not an AC; answer before PLANNED -> RED).**
Before the scope is chosen, a timed date of an "At time" series with All day
switched on shows "At 09:00 on the day". If the owner then answers "This
event only", the series keeps 0 (CAL-057 amendment 2), so that date reminds at
its midnight start, not 09:00. The sheet cannot know the scope while the
owner edits. Options: (a) accept as is; (b) show no helper on an occurrence
until the scope is known; (c) word the helper conditionally. If (b) or (c),
add an AC here before RED.

Required gate that would fail if this broke: `unit`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given a timed weekly series with a 15-minute reminder and one of
  its timed dates open in the sheet, when the owner switches All day on,
  picks "At time", saves and answers "This event only", then `setOverride`
  carries `allDay: true` and `setReminder` is called once with
  `(series id, 0)` - the series' timed "At time", not -540. A test fails under
  `415s#thisOnly ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay#sheetAllDay#` on `EventSheet.tsx`.
- **AC-2** - Given a timed date of a series whose reminder is "At time" (0)
  open in the sheet, when the owner switches All day on, then the Reminder
  select is described by "At 09:00 on the day"; when they switch it back off,
  the helper is gone. A test fails with the field's `allDay` held at the
  stored series basis on an occurrence (RED names the exact mutant; review 4
  used `allDay: occurrence ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay` in substance).
- **AC-3** - Given the reminders read model, then one exported predicate,
  `isLiveTemplate(reminder)` in `src/sync/remindersStore.ts` (true exactly
  when `occurrenceDate === null` and `deletedAt == null`), is what both
  `liveTemplate` and `CalendarPage`'s chip map use. A test of
  `isLiveTemplate` fails at import until it exists, and a test fails when the
  page's map keeps a cleared template (`deletedAt` set).
