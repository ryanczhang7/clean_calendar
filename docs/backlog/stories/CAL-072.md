## Context

EPIC-07. Follow-up to CAL-057 (the event sheet's Reminder field), filed at
the user's request ("Yes, file it") from CAL-057's `## Diff review`, review 4
(`Verdict: Ship.`, no code fix owed) and review 1's Nice to have 2. Line
numbers were re-located at PLANNED on `main` at `352f2ef` (2026-11-19); the
counts quoted from review 4 (136/136, 269/269, "fails 2 at
`EventSheet.test.tsx:2420`") are review 4's, measured at `f393324`, and were
not re-measured.

CAL-057 Block 4's three amendments settle how an "At time" reminder (0 timed,
-540 all-day) behaves on a repeating event, where the reminder is the
series' one template but a single date can be overridden to all-day. Two
lines that carry that rule have no test that fails when they are wrong:

- **The "This event only" basis** (`src/events/EventSheet.tsx:415`, `const
  basis = thisOnly ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay;`). Review 4's probe
  `s/thisOnly ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay/sheetAllDay/` passed 136/136. The
  unpinned input: a timed series (15-minute template), a timed date, All day
  switched on, "At time" picked, "This event only" - today
  `setReminder(series, 0)`; mutated, -540, so every other timed date reminds 9
  hours after its start.
- **The helper's basis after an All-day switch on an occurrence**
  (`EventSheet.tsx:602`, `allDay: sheetAllDay,`). Review 4's probe keeping the
  stored series basis on an occurrence passed 269/269 under `src/events`. A
  timed date of an "At time" series with All day switched on could lose its
  "At 09:00 on the day" helper, or keep one when switched back, with nothing
  red. (The plain-event basis is pinned: `allDay: storedAllDay` fails 2 at
  `EventSheet.test.tsx:2420`, per review 4.)

And one cleanup review 1 asked for: the live-template rule (`occurrenceDate
=== null && deletedAt == null`) is written twice - `liveTemplate`
(`src/sync/remindersStore.ts:32-44`, the rule at `:40-41`) and the page's map
(`useChipReminders`, `src/calendar/CalendarPage.tsx:615-628`, the rule at
`:623`). If one changes and the other does not, the chip's bell and the
sheet's field disagree about the same event.

**PO question, ANSWERED (PO decision 1, 2026-11-19: (a) accept as is; no AC
added).** Before the scope is chosen, a timed date of an "At time" series
with All day switched on shows "At 09:00 on the day". If the owner then
answers "This event only", the series keeps 0 (CAL-057 amendment 2), so that
date reminds at its midnight start, not 09:00. The sheet cannot know the
scope while the owner edits. The options were: (a) accept as is; (b) show no
helper on an occurrence until the scope is known; (c) word the helper
conditionally. The user chose (a) in chat, so AC-2 pins the helper exactly as
it behaves today and no rule of CAL-057's changes.

Required gate that would fail if this broke: `unit` (`required`, covers
`src/**`; every file this story writes is under `src/`). `required_gates`
stays `[]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given a timed weekly series with a 15-minute reminder and one of
  its timed dates open in the sheet, when the owner switches All day on,
  picks "At time", saves and answers "This event only", then `setOverride`
  carries `allDay: true` and `setReminder` is called once with
  `(series id, 0)` - the series' timed "At time", not -540. A test fails under
  `415s#thisOnly ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay#sheetAllDay#` on `EventSheet.tsx`.
- **AC-2** - Given a timed date of a timed series whose reminder is "At time"
  (0) open in the sheet, when the owner switches All day on, then the Reminder
  select is described by "At 09:00 on the day"; when they switch it back off,
  the helper is gone. A test fails under
  `602s#allDay: sheetAllDay,#allDay: occurrence ? storedAllDay : sheetAllDay,#`
  on `EventSheet.tsx` - the field's `allDay` held at the stored series basis
  on an occurrence.
- **AC-3** - Given the reminders read model, then one exported predicate,
  `isLiveTemplate(reminder)` in `src/sync/remindersStore.ts` (true exactly
  when `occurrenceDate === null` and `deletedAt == null`), is what both
  `liveTemplate` and `CalendarPage`'s chip map use. A test of
  `isLiveTemplate` fails for the named absence until it exists, and a test
  fails when the page's map keeps a cleared template (`deletedAt` set).

### Block 3 - `isLiveTemplate` (AC-3, MECHANICAL)

In `src/sync/remindersStore.ts`, exported, beside `liveTemplate`:

```ts
/** A live template: `occurrenceDate` null and `deletedAt == null`. */
export function isLiveTemplate(reminder: Reminder): boolean;
```

`Reminder` is the type already imported there from `../data/rows`. It reads
two fields and nothing else - never `eventId`, `sentAt` or `minutesBefore`.

| Row | `isLiveTemplate` | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `occurrenceDate: null`, `deletedAt: null` | `true` | the live template |
| `occurrenceDate: null`, `deletedAt` a timestamp | `false` | a cleared template |
| `occurrenceDate: "2026-10-27"`, `deletedAt: null` | `false` | a per-occurrence row |
| `occurrenceDate: "2026-10-27"`, `deletedAt` a timestamp | `false` | both |
| `occurrenceDate: null`, no `deletedAt` key at all | `true` | a row streamed before CAL-026; `== null`, not `=== null` (the existing `liveTemplate` case at `remindersStore.test.ts:96-98` builds one) |

RED's cases go in `src/sync/remindersStore.test.ts`, with the fixtures already
at its top (`template`, `cleared`, `materialised`). **The named absence:** in
RED `isLiveTemplate` is not exported. Under vitest a missing named export is
expected to arrive as `undefined`, so the new cases fail with a `TypeError`
("is not a function") while the file's existing cases still pass, and the
`typecheck` gate fails with TS2305 on that import; RED records which it
actually saw. Either is the right failure; an assertion failure is not.

GREEN, the minimum:

- `liveTemplate(reminders, eventId)` (signature unchanged) finds
  `r.eventId === eventId && isLiveTemplate(r)`.
- `useChipReminders` in `src/calendar/CalendarPage.tsx` (module-private,
  `:615`, its one call at `:666`) replaces line 623's condition with
  `if (isLiveTemplate(r)) {` - the loop variable stays `r` and the call is
  spelt exactly so, because Deferred verification 1 addresses it by that

  import at `:41`).
- After GREEN the text `occurrenceDate === null` appears once across those
  two source files, inside `isLiveTemplate` (Deferred verification 2).

What no unit test can tell apart is a call to `isLiveTemplate` from an
inline copy of its body - that is the whole defect. So "both use it" is
