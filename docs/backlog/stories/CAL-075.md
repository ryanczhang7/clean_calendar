## Context

EPIC-06 (recurring events). CAL-074 made `focusChip` in
`src/calendar/CalendarPage.tsx` find the chip to refocus after a keyboard move
by `data-chip-key`, i.e. `chipKey(event)` (`<series id>@<occurrenceDate>`) of
the occurrence AS IT WAS BEFORE the move. That is right for a "This event
only" move (the override stays keyed by the original occurrence date) and for
a plain event (the key is the id).

It is wrong for an "All events in the series" keyboard move that shifts the
series by whole days: Left/Right (`commandSpan`, one day) in any view, or
Up/Down in the month view (seven days). `seriesSpan` shifts the series row, so
the moved occurrence is redrawn with an `occurrenceDate` that many days later
and the pre-move key names either no chip (a weekly series: focus is lost) or
the chip of a different occurrence (a daily series: the one that now falls on
the old date). Before CAL-074 focus went to the series' first chip in the
document. Found by CAL-074's GREEN Feature Developer and confirmed by the
orchestrator by reading `commandSpan` and the series branch of the scope
confirm (`CalendarPage.tsx`, `if (ok && asked.focusAfter) focusChip(event)`).
Reasoned from the code, not yet observed in a test: RED watches each
criterion fail.

This is a `fix`: each criterion reproduces the defect. The required gate that
fails if it breaks is `unit` (and `coverage`): jsdom tests in
`src/calendar/CalendarPage.test.tsx`, beside CAL-025's
'"All events in the series" on a move' describe. No optional gate is needed.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** — Given a weekly series on Wednesdays (CAL-025's "Spin", weekly from
  Wednesday 4 November 2026) in the month view of November 2026, when the
  owner focuses the chip on Wednesday 18 November, presses Alt+ArrowRight and
  confirms "All events in the series", then keyboard focus is on the Spin chip
  in Thursday 19 November's cell. (Today focus is lost: no chip is keyed
  `@2026-11-18` any more.)
- **AC-2** — Given the same series in the month view, when the owner focuses
  the chip on Wednesday 11 November, presses Alt+ArrowDown (seven days) and
  confirms "All events in the series", then keyboard focus is on the Spin
  chip in Wednesday 18 November's cell - the occurrence that was 11 November.
  (Today focus lands in the 11 November cell: the series now starts there, so
  a chip keyed `@2026-11-11` exists, but it is the occurrence that was
  4 November.)
- **AC-3** — Given a daily series in the week view, when the owner focuses its
  occurrence on Tuesday, presses Alt+ArrowRight and confirms "All events in the
  series", then keyboard focus is on the chip of the occurrence now on
  Wednesday (the moved one), not the chip on Tuesday. (Today focus lands on
  Tuesday's chip, which is the occurrence that was Monday's.)
- **AC-4** — Given the same fixtures, when the move is confirmed "This event
  only" instead, then focus is on the moved occurrence's chip, as today
  (CAL-074's behaviour kept). A regression guard for the scope this story
  must not break.

## Out of scope

- **Pointer drag of a whole series.** A drag ends with no `focusAfter`; focus
  after a drop is not this defect.
- **Month view: one overnight occurrence in two cells.** Which of the two cells
  is focused is not decided here (CAL-074 Out of scope).
- **The deep-link time-zone tie-break** (CAL-058 diff-review finding 2) and the
  **overlap layout keyed by id** (`src/domain/timeGrid.ts`): both still without
  a story, as CAL-074's Out of scope says.
- **A moved series whose new first occurrence falls outside the visible
  period.** If the moved occurrence is not on screen after the move, focus
  falls back to whatever CAL-013 does for an off-screen move; no criterion
  here.
- No change to `seriesSpan`, `commandSpan`, the scope dialog, `chipKey`,
  `EventChip` or `TimeGrid`.
