EPIC-06. Source: CAL-079's `## Notes`, "Found while measuring" (2026-11-20),
filed on the user's answer of 2026-11-21 ("File a story"). Bounded by CAL-023
Block 3 (how a wall time that does not exist resolves), CAL-078 (the end keeps
the series' length on the wall clock of the series' zone) and CAL-079 Block 2
(an all-day occurrence whose start falls in a gap still ends on the wall
rule).

`expand` in `shared/recurrence.ts` reads a series' length off the stored row
once, as `endWall - startWall` on the wall clock of `recurrence.tz`, and gives
that length to every later occurrence. `toInstants` in `src/domain/eventForm.ts`
stores an all-day row as local midnight to the next local midnight. In a zone
whose clocks go forward **at midnight**, one midnight a year does not exist,
and a row stored beside it does not read as 24 hours on the wall. Cuba is the
measured zone: on 8 March 2026, 00:00 CST becomes 01:00 CDT at
`2026-03-08T05:00:00.000Z`, so local 8 March begins at 01:00.

Measured on `main` at `cca4e1c`, Node 24.19.0, host zone America/Chicago, all
instants UTC:

- **Row stored on the day before (7 March).** `2026-03-07T05:00Z` to
  `2026-03-08T05:00Z`, all-day, `America/Havana`. The row ends at 01:00 on the
  wall, a 25-hour wall length. Daily: the 8 March occurrence ends
  `2026-03-09T05:00Z`, the 9 March one `2026-03-10T05:00Z`, the 10 March one
  `2026-03-11T05:00Z`: each ends at 01:00 the next local day, so a range that
  is exactly local 9 March returns two occurrences, 8 March's and 9 March's.
  Weekly: 14 March ends `2026-03-15T05:00Z`. Yearly: 7 March 2027 ends
  `2027-03-08T06:00Z` (Cuba's clocks go forward on 14 March in 2027, so that
  is also 01:00 on the 8th).
- **Row stored on the day itself (8 March).** `2026-03-08T05:00Z` to

**Block 2 - the rule (PO-1, candidate a, the user, 2026-11-21).** Terms, all
in `recurrence.tz`:

- The **day start** of a wall time `w` is `w` with its time of day removed:
  `Math.floor(w / DAY_MS) * DAY_MS`, a `Wall`.
- The **first instant** of a local date is `fromWall(<that date's 00:00
  wall>, f)`. Where 00:00 exists once it is local midnight. Where it exists
  twice (a fall-back onto midnight, Havana on 1 November 2026) `fromWall`
  gives the earlier one, which is the first. Where it does not exist (Havana
  on 8 March 2026, 00:00 CST becomes 01:00 CDT) `fromWall` falls back to the
  pre-transition offset (`:103`, CAL-023 Block 3): 00:00 at -05:00 is
  `2026-03-08T05:00:00.000Z`, the instant the clocks change, which a clock
  there shows as 01:00 CDT. That is the first instant of 8 March. No new
  gap handling is written.
- An instant `t` **is a first instant** when
  `fromWall(dayStart(toWall(t, f)), f) === t`.
- A series is a **whole-day row** when `series.allDay` is true and both
  `series.startsAt` and `series.endsAt` are first instants. Every row
  `toInstants` writes for an all-day event is one (local midnight to the
  local midnight after the last day, on the device whose zone is `tz`); H7
  and H8 are too, H7's end and H8's start being the 01:00 first instant of
  8 March.
- A whole-day row's **count of dates** is
  `days = (dayStart(endWall) - dayStart(startWall)) / DAY_MS`, read off the
  row once, where `startWall` and `endWall` are today's `:135`-`:136`. H7:
  7 March 00:00 to 8 March 01:00 on the wall, `days = 1` (its wall length is
  25 h). H8: 8 March 01:00 to 9 March 00:00, `days = 1` (wall length 23 h).
  The control row (6 to 7 March) and every ordinary one-day row: `1`. A
  three-day row 5 to 7 March, `2026-03-05T05:00Z` to `2026-03-08T05:00Z`:
  `3`.

The rule, for `k > 0` only:

- Whole-day row: `start = fromWall(dayStart(sWall), f)` and
  `end = fromWall(dayStart(sWall) + days * DAY_MS, f)`: the occurrence runs
  from the first instant of its own date to the first instant of the date
  `days` later. `sWall` is today's `shift(startWall, freq, k)` (`:145`), so
  the date, `occurrenceDate` (`:147`), the `until` test (`:148`) and the
  29 February `null` (`:146`) are untouched; H8's `sWall` carries 01:00 and
  `dayStart` removes it.
- Every other series, which is every timed row and any all-day row whose
  stored instants are not both first instants in `tz` (the form never writes
  one): today's code, unchanged, lines `:155`-`:167`, CAL-079's in-gap branch
  included.
- Occurrence 0 (`k === 0`, `:151`-`:153`) stays the row's own two instants
  for every series, read from `series.startsAt` and `series.endsAt`, never
  recomputed (AC-5).
- `allDay` is the series row's, never an override's; overrides are applied
  after this, as today.

**Why the whole-day condition and not `series.allDay` alone.** For a
whole-day row away from a midnight change, `startWall` and `endWall` are
both 00:00 on the wall, so `dayStart(sWall) === sWall` and
`days * DAY_MS === endWall - startWall`: both lines above reduce to today's
`:155` and `:166` and the result is identical by construction (the user's
"identical to today"). An all-day row that is not midnight-aligned in `tz`
is left on today's code so that it too is identical to today; measured
below.
